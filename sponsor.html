What you'll get

The Warden's update report

Sponsors get periodic reports from the Warden's office on how their inmate is serving their time. A sample from the files:

Good boy
Last Chance Ranch · Department of PawctionsInmate Update ReportCase No. 34 · Subject: "Blue"
Behavior report
Subject was observed completing 47 laps of the yard for no documented reason. When questioned, subject wagged. Investigation closed.
Commissary log
Your funds were disbursed as follows: one (1) bag of kibble, one (1) heartworm preventative, one (1) tennis ball — since confiscated from under his bed, alongside the three we did not purchase.
Medical record
Clean bill of health. The vet described him as "a lot," which we are entering into the file as a compliment.
Parole status
Still eligible. Still waiting. Still absolutely certain today is the day. Your support keeps his tail at regulation wag while he waits.

Reports are written by actual humans about your actual dog. Frequency varies with rescue chaos.
