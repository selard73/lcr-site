Sponsor an inmate

Put a dog on your payroll

Can't adopt right now? Sponsor an inmate instead. Your monthly gift covers one dog's food, meds, and vet care while they wait for parole — and the Warden keeps you posted on how your dog is doing.

Start — pick your inmate

Step one

Pick your inmate

Look through the files and tap your dog. Long-timers and sanctuary residents need sponsors most — they're in it for the long haul.

Step two

Choose your dog's support

Suggested levels — every dollar goes to your dog's care. Pick one, then choose monthly or one-time.

$10

Commissary snacks

Treats, chews, and the occasional contraband tennis ball.

$25

Room & board

A month of food and heartworm & flea prevention.

$50

Full sentence support

Food plus a real dent in vet care for the dogs who wait longest.

Any

Care package

You pick the amount in Cash App — every bit helps.

Step three

Get on the visitor list

Tell us where to send your dog's update reports from the Warden's office.

Step four

Sponsorship filed

Filed

Your dog has been notified of their new benefactor. (There was wagging.)

One last thing — the money part happens in Cash App:

  1. Tap the button below — it opens Cash App.
  2. Put SPONSOR YOUR DOG in the note so we match the payment to you.
  3. Set it to repeat monthly in Cash App.
Open Cash App

The Warden's office will confirm by email and your first Inmate Update Report will follow. Thank you — you just made a dog's whole month.
