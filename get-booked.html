Get booked

Every pet is guilty of something. Upload their photo, receive their official Last Chance Ranch mugshot card, and post it proudly. Free — but real inmates are counting on your bail money.

On a phone: move & zoom with two fingers, or press and hold a moment, then drag with one finger (the card lights up red when it’s ready). One quick finger just scrolls the page. On a computer: drag with the mouse, size with the slider.

By pinning, you confirm this is your pet's photo (no people, please) and agree the finished card may be shown in the public Booking Log below and on Last Chance Ranch's social media. The Warden reviews every card before it appears.
