Warden's notes

Inmate #51 officially checked into Last Chance Ranch from Orangeburg, SC, and immediately established himself as a professional. Leroy already knows all of his basic commands. He is smart, attentive, and does his best work alongside one trusted human — the kind of partner who's already thought three moves ahead and doesn't need a crew.

Now, every inmate has a few conditions to their parole. Leroy's is simple: he prefers to be the only inmate in the cell block. He is not dog-friendly or cat-friendly — but he is incredibly loyal and devoted to his human. Once you earn his trust, you've got a best friend for life. That's not a limitation. That's a commitment.

(PAROLE) recommended to a one-dog household ready for a partner, not a pet.

Conditions of paroleOnly pet in the home. A person ready to earn his trust and keep it.
