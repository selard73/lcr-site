Warden's notes

The Warden reports that H-Block remains under the firm control of its resident sibling duo, Inmate #8 Lizzy and Inmate #9 Thumper. These two have perfected the art of sibling life: one minute inseparable, the next arguing over who gets the best bed, five minutes later back together like nothing happened. All disagreements are immediately forgotten if treats are involved.

They may bicker like siblings, but Lizzy and Thumper are a bonded pair and must be adopted together. They've spent their lives side by side, and separating them isn't an option. They deserve a home where they can keep serving their sentence together — keeping morale high and the snack budget completely out of control.

Case status: guilty of stealing hearts. (PAROLE) recommended — together.

Conditions of paroleAdopted together with Thumper (#9). Two beds. A generous snack budget.
