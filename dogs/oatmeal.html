Warden's notes

Inmate #39 has been in the system since May 28, 2024 — longer than any dog should wait. He's currently doing his time in the Half Way House (a private home, home-to-home adoption), where he has taken up a quiet line of work: greeting people at doors like every one of them is the score of a lifetime.

He is a model inmate — gets along with dogs, gets along with cats, guards nothing but the doorway. He is still perfecting his house training and pulls a little on leash for the first block, then settles in like a pro. He doesn't need perfection. He needs a patient human and a soft place to land.

Booked 05/28/24. He has served his time. (PAROLE) is strongly recommended.

Conditions of paroleA patient home willing to finish house training. Home-to-home adoption — he goes straight from his foster home to yours.
