Warden's notes

Inmate #35 is what the file calls a smooth operator. Isaiah has stolen the hearts of staff and inmates alike with a charming personality, an irresistible smile, and cuddle skills that should honestly require a permit. He is always on his best behavior when treats or toys are involved — and he has figured out that treats or toys are always involved if he looks at you like that.

He is friendly with people, friendly with dogs, and merely interested in cats, which around here counts as restraint. Assigned rank: Snuggle Officer. He takes the title seriously.

Isaiah is ready for parole into a loving forever home. (PAROLE) is highly recommended.

Conditions of paroleAn approved adopter willing to provide structure, exercise, treats, and a lifelong commitment.
